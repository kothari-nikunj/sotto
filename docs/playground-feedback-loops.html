    Numbered task selection
    The existing cleanup tool accepts typed all-except selection on a complete displayed open list (global or exact requested subset), or explicit scalar/range completion and leave-open clauses. Strict completion checks survive sentence recursion and refuse unreferenced numeric choices. Runtime and receiver bind the exact owner text to the immediately prior accepted list and derive its frozen indices. Excluded, explicitly open and unmentioned rows stay untouched; no keep writer is called for them. Source, identity, route and row-version checks remain, and partial or uncertain effects keep their existing recovery. An intervening owner turn requires a fresh list. Typed spans support this bounded contract, not arbitrary natural-language authority. A wholly applied resolve-only batch larger than five items uses one completion-count sentence; other outcomes retain per-item detail.
