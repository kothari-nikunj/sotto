The machine, explorable

Two processes, five modules, four daemon threads, sixteen skills, and one funnel that decides whether your phone buzzes. Click a piece to open its dossier; the funnel at the bottom will walk a real event through its gates and tell you what happened, and why.

A Mac that can read your messages, and a container that can think. They never share a network — the Mac pushes out and long-polls back, so nothing at home needs an open port.

The shared fileswriter → reader

Every JSON write goes through connectors.write_json (tmp at 0600 → os.replace), so a crash mid-write can never leave a torn file. Every row but the last is one-way: exactly one writer, readers that never write. That is the property that keeps the two processes from needing a lock.

Act III

The skills spine

The container half is the plumbing. The product is the skills tree — and every Sotto behaviour is the same five-step spine. Deterministic Python does the gathering and the learning; one Gemini call composes; a validator rejects a malformed or hallucinated brief before it reaches you. The LLM writes prose — it never decides whether to interrupt you.

Pick a step of the spine.

The sixteen skills

Where the schedule lives: adapters/hermes/crons.json is the ONE source for the cron jobs — morning brief {cron.morning}, midday digest {cron.midday}, evening brief {cron.evening}, relationship pulse {cron.pulse_day} {cron.pulse_time}, proactive check every {cron.proactive_min} minutes. Four registrars read that file; none of them keeps its own copy.

Act IV — the interactive one

The funnel

Every event — a text, a WhatsApp message, a call, an email the container polled itself, a meeting that just ended — is deduped by (source, rowid) and runs through one funnel, synchronously. Tier 0 is deterministic and free. Tier 1 is one cheap LLM call on whatever survives. Any Tier-1 error queues — the funnel fails toward silence, never toward noise.
